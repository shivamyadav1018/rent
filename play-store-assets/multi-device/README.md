# KirayaBahi multi-device Play Store screenshots

`upload-ready` contains four images for each Play Console device category:

- `phone`: 1080 × 1920, portrait 9:16
- `phone-v2`: 1080 × 1920, portrait 9:16, enlarged high-legibility layout
- `7-inch-tablet`: 1440 × 2560, portrait 9:16
- `10-inch-tablet`: 2560 × 1440, landscape 16:9
- `chromebook`: 2560 × 1440, landscape 16:9

The assets use live screenshots captured from the logged-in KirayaBahi build. Personal greeting and phone-number areas are replaced in the publishable compositions. Raw captures under `source-screens` are working files and must not be uploaded.

The sequence is consistent across device categories:

1. Dashboard and rent collection summary
2. Tenant records and status
3. Monthly ledger
4. Properties and units

Google Play currently accepts up to eight screenshots per supported device type. For large screens, Google recommends at least four screenshots, dimensions from 1080 to 7680 pixels, and 9:16 portrait or 16:9 landscape.
